My three(3) Planned projects

These are three projects I plan to develop as I continue learning software development.
They are inspired by my experience in education and my desire to use technology to solve everyday challenges.

First Project: Kenya Education Management Information System(KEMIS)

This project will help schools manage learner records, attendance, and basic academic information in one place.
The goal is to reduce manual paperwork and make it easier for teachers and administrators to access important information quickly.

Second Project: E-learning Support Platform

This platform will provide learners with access to learning materials, simple quizzes, and teacher support.
It is designed especially for learners who may not always have access to physical learning resources, helping to make learning more flexible and inclusive.

Third Project: Stakeholders Education Feedback Application

This application will allow students, teachers, parents and other education stakeholders to share feedback about learning experiences and school services.
The feedback collected can help schools identify challenges early and improve the quality of education delivery.
