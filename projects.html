My Projects

These are planned projects that reflect my research interests in artificial intelligence, simulation, edge computing, education systems, and research methodology.

Planned

1. Kenya Education Management Information System (KEMIS)

Ministry of Education management information system for decision-making.

This project will help schools manage learner records, attendance, and basic academic information in one place.

The goal is to reduce manual paperwork and make it easier for teachers and administrators to access important information quickly.

2. E-Learning Support Platform

Platform for supporting e-learning initiatives and resources.

This platform will provide learners with access to learning materials, simple quizzes, and teacher support.

It is designed especially for learners who may not always have access to physical learning resources, helping to make learning more flexible and inclusive.

3. Data Analytics Dashboard for Students

Planned dashboard to support education planning and reporting.

This project will create a simple web dashboard to help education stakeholders plan, coordinate, and report on student data for decision-making purposes.

It will provide tools for documenting and recording education data and project timelines.
