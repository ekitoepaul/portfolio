My Background

I am a teacher by profession.
Currently I serve as a quality assurance & standards officer in the ministry of education,Nairobi -Kenya.
My work has given me a strong interest in using technology to improve education.
Over the years, I have worked closely with learners,teachers, other education stakeholders and education systems.
I am therefore,learning software Engineering to build practical digital solutions that can support teaching, learning, and Kenya education management Information stystem.
This experience has shown me how powerful technology can be in improving the quality of education in my country -Kenya.

Why I am Learning Development

I am learning software development because I want to do more than use technology; I want to create solutions that make a real difference.
I am especially interested in building simple, practical tools that can support teaching, learning, and education management.
Every new skill I learn brings me closer to turning ideas into useful digital solutions.
