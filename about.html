About Me

My Background

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Maseno University with a strong interest in developing education systems, simulation and modelling, artificial intelligence, and edge computing.

My academic work combines theoretical understanding with practical research design, data analysis, and computational experimentation.

Currently, I serve as a Quality Assurance and Standards Officer in the Ministry of Education, Nairobi, Kenya. My work has given me a strong interest in using technology to improve education.

Over the years, I have worked closely with learners, teachers, education stakeholders, and education systems. I am therefore learning software engineering to build practical digital solutions that can support teaching, learning, and Kenya's education management information systems.

This experience has shown me how powerful technology can be in improving the quality of education in Kenya.

Why I Am Learning to Code

I am learning to code because programming allows me to build tools that test ideas, automate analysis, and model complex systems. It also helps me turn research questions into working prototypes and communicate results through interactive web-based portfolios.

Skills

  • HTML and semantic web development
  • Python programming for data analysis and simulation
  • Research design and experimental methods
  • Artificial intelligence and machine-learning concepts
  • Edge computing and distributed systems
  • Academic writing, presentation, and supervision

Goals

My goal is to complete doctoral research that contributes to intelligent, efficient, and ethical edge-computing systems. I also aim to develop practical software tools, publish high-quality research, and support other students in developing strong research and technical skills.
